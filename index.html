BA vs PM at a glance

Two roles, one outcome. Knowing where they differ makes handoffs smoother.

Business AnalystProject Manager
Core questionWhat problem are we solving, and what must the solution do?How, when and at what cost will we deliver it?
Main outputRequirements, models, business case, acceptance criteriaPlan, schedule, budget, risk register, status reports
StandardBABOK Guide (IIBA)PMBOK Guide (PMI)
CertificationsECBA, CCBA, CBAPCAPM, PMP
Success measureSolution delivers the intended business valueOn time, on budget, in scope, with quality

Example: fixing stock discrepancies

Role boundaries vary between organizations. This is one common split.

ActivityUsually led byCollaboration
Investigate why stock counts differBAPM schedules time with warehouse staff
Define inventory visibility requirementsBAPM confirms scope and budget fit
Build the delivery plan, milestones and dependenciesPMBA flags requirement-driven dependencies
Manage risks and vendor timelinesPMBA assesses impact on requirements
Assess a change request for scannersSharedBA: business impact. PM: time and cost impact
Plan and run UAT, then sign-offSharedBA: scenarios. PM: schedule and approvals
